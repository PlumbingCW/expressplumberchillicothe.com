Plumber in Hesswood Hills, Chillicothe, OH.

Hesswood Hills is a residential neighborhood on the northwest side of Chillicothe, built around Applewood Drive and its loops of quiet streets. When a water heater, drain or pipe fails here, call (866) 310-6010 and tell us your street.

This page explains what we know about the neighborhood from public sources, what plumbing questions come with homes of this kind, and how to describe your problem so the call is quick.

Hesswood Hills on the map.

Real estate directories place Hesswood Hills on the northwest side of Chillicothe, in ZIP code 45601, and describe a neighborhood of quiet streets, mature trees and single-family homes. The City of Chillicothe's ward map shows the main streets, including Applewood Drive, Ledgewood Drive, Timberlane Drive, Watch Hill Drive and Briarcliff Drive.

Other streets reported for the neighborhood include Eastview Avenue, Knollwood Lane, Linwood Drive, Nelson Drive, Oakwood Drive, Overlook Drive, Randall Drive and Uhlan Court. Boundaries are not documented by an official source, so we describe the area by these anchor streets, not by a hard line.

Nearby landmarks named in the directories include Plyleys Lane, Western Avenue and the Chillicothe Country Club.

Plumbing questions that suit this kind of neighborhood.

Detached homes on larger lots raise some practical questions. These are general points, not findings about any Hesswood Hills address.

  • A water heater that has been in service for many years and is leaking or slow to heat. See water heater repair and replacement..
  • A longer run of drain pipe between the house and the street, where roots or a belly can slow flow. See sewer line cleaning..
  • A basement or lower level that takes on water after heavy rain. See sump pump repair..
  • Outdoor spigots and a hose bib that need winter attention. See burst pipe repair..

Hills, rain and basements.

The directories describe Hesswood Hills as sitting among rolling hills with winding streets. Sloped lots send surface water somewhere, and where it goes after a storm matters to a basement.

If water appears at the floor edge during or after heavy rain, a pump and a clear discharge line are the first things to check. Make sure the discharge pipe empties well away from the foundation. If you hear the pump running constantly, call before it burns out.

Water and sewer for a city address.

If your Hesswood Hills address is served by the City of Chillicothe, the city's Water & Sewer department handles the public mains, and your own service line and lateral are private. Ross County Planning notes a county program, Community Housing Impact and Preservation (CHIP), that lists plumbing and water heaters among eligible repairs for income-eligible homeowners. It is a countywide program, so check current availability with Ross County Planning & Development.

Rules and sources for this page.

A water heater replacement has to meet the Ohio Plumbing Code, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), including a temperature and pressure relief valve with a safe discharge. Gas storage heaters are built to ANSI Z21.10.1 and electric ones to UL 174. Ohio licenses plumbing contractors under Chapter 4740 of the Revised Code, so ask any contractor for their license details.

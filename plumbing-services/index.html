How to choose the right service.

Start with what you see. Water on the floor leads to leak detection or burst pipe repair. A slow or backing-up drain leads to drain cleaning and, for repeat clogs, hydro jetting. A cold shower leads to water heater repair.

If you are planning work rather than reacting to a problem, the pages for water heater installation, repiping and plumbing installation cover the process and the choices.

Specialty pages for regional needs.

Some pages exist because Ross County's ground and water call for them. Sump pump service and basement waterproofing respond to groundwater near the Scioto and Paint Creek. Water softener and filtration responds to moderately hard groundwater. Boiler repair serves older homes with radiators.

What we do not do.

We do not pump septic tanks, remove mold or service large industrial systems. If your problem sits outside our service list, we say so and suggest where to start. See also our service area for the towns we cover.

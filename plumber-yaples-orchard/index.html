Plumber in Yaples Orchard, Chillicothe, OH.

Yaples Orchard is a residential subdivision on the west side of Chillicothe, built on the site of a former Ross County apple orchard. If a water heater, filter or drain needs attention, call (866) 310-6010 and give your street.

The homes here are larger and, by most descriptions, newer than the city's older neighborhoods. That changes the questions we ask when you call.

A subdivision that remembers its orchard.

Real estate directories describe Yaples Orchard as a quiet residential area on the western edge of Chillicothe, in ZIP code 45601, on land that was once a large Ross County apple orchard. The street names carry the history: Winesap Court, McIntosh Court and Melrose Drive are all apple varieties.

The streets reported for the subdivision are Yaples Orchard Drive, Avalon Drive, Baldwin Court, McIntosh Court, Melrose Drive, Suncrest Court and Winesap Court. Several are cul-de-sacs. The directories place it a short drive from the Adena Health System campus, North High Street and the Unioto school campus.

No official source documents an exact boundary, so treat the street list as a guide.

Newer, bigger homes raise different questions.

Larger custom homes tend to have more bathrooms, longer pipe runs and bigger water heaters than a small older house. That is general plumbing information, not a finding about any one address.

In practice it means a few questions are worth asking. How long does hot water take to reach the farthest bathroom? Is the heater sized for the household? Is there a recirculation line? Our water heater installation page and tankless water heater page explain the options and the trade-offs.

Ask also about whole-house filtration or a softener. Our water softener and filtration page covers when treatment makes sense and when a water test shows you do not need it.

When to call quickly.

Do not wait on these, whatever the age of the house.

  • Water is running and you cannot stop it at the fixture. Close the main shutoff..
  • A drain is backing up into a tub or floor drain..
  • The water heater is leaking from the tank itself..
  • You smell gas. Leave the house and call 911 before you call anyone else..

Which water and sewer service do you have?

Some west-side addresses are served by the City of Chillicothe, and others by different providers depending on location. If you are not sure who supplies your water or takes your sewer, ask the Ross County Auditor or your latest bill. That answer decides who handles a main-line problem and who is responsible for the pipes at your property.

Rules and sources for this page.

Water treatment products that touch drinking water are tested to NSF/ANSI standards, including NSF/ANSI 44 for softeners, 58 for reverse osmosis and 61 for system components. Gas water heaters are built to ANSI Z21.10.1, and Ohio licenses plumbing contractors under Chapter 4740 of the Revised Code.

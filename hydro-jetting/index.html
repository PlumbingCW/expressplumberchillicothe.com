What hydro jetting is and how it differs from a snake.

Hydro jetting sends water through a flexible hose and a nozzle at high pressure. The jets cut grease, scale and soft roots from the pipe wall and flush the debris toward the sewer. A snake pokes a hole through the middle of a clog and leaves the film on the sides.

A line that has been snaked three times in a year usually needs a jet. A line with a single new clog usually does not.

Jetting is not the right tool for a pipe that is cracked, collapsed or badly corroded. That is why a camera check comes first.

When jetting makes sense.

These patterns tell us a jet is worth the cost.

  • The same drain has clogged more than once in a year..
  • Slow drains are paired with a greasy smell, especially in kitchens..
  • A camera has shown heavy buildup or roots without structural damage..
  • A restaurant, laundry or multi-unit building has a line that carries heavy waste..
  • A cable cleans the line, but it clogs again within weeks..

Roots, grease and mineral scale.

Grease cools and sticks in kitchen drains and builds up past the disposal. In a restaurant or a multi-unit building it can close a four-inch line in a year.

Roots enter at joints and fill the pipe with hair-like growth. Jetting removes them, but unless the joint is sealed or lined, they return.

Mineral scale in cast iron narrows the bore from the inside. A jet breaks off the loose layers. If the pipe underneath is thin, we say so before the jet makes it worse.

Hydro jetting for commercial and multi-unit lines.

Shared lines in apartment buildings and in commercial kitchens see steady grease and solids. A scheduled jet every year or two costs less than a weekend backup.

We discuss a maintenance schedule if you manage such property, and refer to our commercial plumbing page for after-hours service.

Jetting in older city lines.

Chillicothe's downtown buildings, such as those near the Majestic Theatre and the Ross County Courthouse, are old, and some drain lines have been patched many times. Before jetting any older line, we inspect it with a camera, because pressure that cleans a sound pipe can open a weak one.

For restaurants along the North Bridge Street corridor and downtown, grease management matters. A routine jet schedule keeps a line from failing in the middle of a dinner rush.

Where jetting fits in the code and the pipe.

Jetting is a maintenance method rather than a code item, so the limits come from the pipe. Cast iron, clay and plastic each tolerate different pressures, and a camera inspection comes first to confirm the line is sound. Any repair that follows must meet the Ohio Plumbing Code, built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Chillicothe, OH.

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. Call (866) 310-6010 now and Chillicothe Express Plumbing will talk through an emergency burst pipe repair at your home in Chillicothe, Ohio. Pipes do not break on a schedule, so ask on the call what can be arranged for evenings and weekends.

Close the main water shutoff first. It is usually near where the line enters the house, in a basement, utility room or crawlspace. Then open the lowest faucet to drain the lines, switch off power to wet areas and move valuables out of the water's path.

What to do in the first ten minutes.

These steps limit damage while the plumber is on the way.

01

Shut the main valve.

Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it will not close, tell the person on the phone so we bring a curb key for the street-side shutoff.

02

Drain the lines.

Open the lowest tap in the house and a high one to let air in. Flush a toilet once to empty the tank.

03

If water is near outlets, a panel or a ceiling fixture, turn off those breakers from a dry spot. Do not touch a panel if you are standing in water.

04

Take photos and a short video of the break, the water and the damage. This helps with an insurance claim.

Why pipes burst in Ross County.

Freezing is the number one cause in southern Ohio. Water expands about nine percent as it turns to ice, and the pressure builds between the ice plug and the closed faucet, not at the ice itself. The pipe splits at the weakest point, often along a seam, and the damage only shows once the ice melts.

Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages and under kitchen sinks along outside walls are the common victims. Corrosion, high water pressure and age-related pinholes cause breaks in summer as well.

A pipe that splits in an attic or a basement ceiling can drop water through the floor below, so the visible damage is often far from the break.

Frozen pipes that have not burst yet.

If a faucet gives only a trickle or nothing in freezing weather, a pipe is frozen. Open the tap, and warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer starting near the faucet and working back toward the frozen section. Never use a flame or torch.

If you see frost, bulging or water on the pipe, shut the main and call. A thawed pipe that has split will begin leaking the moment ice melts.

We can also help prevent the next freeze: pipe insulation, heat tape where it is safe, and moving exposed lines away from outside walls.

Pinholes, pipe leaks and cracked drain pipes.

Not every burst is a dramatic spray. Pinhole leaks in copper show as green or white crust and slow drips. They grow gradually and indicate that nearby sections are thin too.

Cracked drain pipes behave differently. They leak only when water runs through them. A damp basement wall or a stain below a bathroom that appears after each shower is a typical sign. We run water through each fixture while we look at the pipe.

If one pipe has failed from corrosion, tell us if other repairs were done in the past. A pattern often means a bigger plan is cheaper.

Repair options and honest trade-offs.

A single split in an otherwise sound line is a spot repair. We cut out the damaged section and connect new pipe with soldered or push-fit couplings. This usually takes a few hours once the water is off.

If the pipe is old, brittle or failing in more than one place, replacing a larger run is cheaper than coming back for each failure. We show you the pipe, explain what we see and quote both options.

After the repair we turn the water back on slowly, bleed air from the lines and check for further leaks before we leave.

Ross County freeze and flood history.

Southern Ohio swings between deep cold snaps and wet thaws. A burst can send water into a basement already damp from groundwater in a city that sits at the meeting of the Scioto River and Paint Creek. A plumber has to look at both.

If your basement floods while a pipe is also broken, shut off electricity to the basement before entering, and tell the person on the phone about both. We will bring a pump and a camera along with the repair kit.

Homeowners policies commonly cover sudden pipe bursts and the resulting water damage, but not the cost of the pipe itself or damage from long-term seepage. Photograph everything, save the failed section of pipe if you can, and call your insurer after the water is off.

What governs a pipe repair.

A repair section has to use approved pipe and fittings under the Ohio Plumbing Code, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Press, solder and push-fit joints each have their own manufacturer instructions. Insurance adjusters usually ask for photos and an invoice, so keep both.

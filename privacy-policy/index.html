Privacy Policy.

This page explains what information is collected when you visit this website or call our number. Last updated November 13, 2026.

What this site collects.

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you to type personal information into the site.

Like most websites, the hosting service may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. These logs are used to run and secure the site.

Phone calls.

When you call (888) 910-8644, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us about your problem and address. We use it to arrange a plumber, give a price and follow up on your service.

Calls may be routed through a call-handling service and may be recorded for quality and training. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the person on the phone at the start of the call.

Maps and third-party content.

Pages include embedded Google Maps. Loading a map may send information such as your IP address to Google under Google's own privacy terms.

How information is shared.

We share call details only with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers who help us take and manage calls. We do not sell your information.

Your choices and contact.

To ask about information we hold from a call, or to ask us to delete it, email contact@expressplumberchillicothe.com.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
